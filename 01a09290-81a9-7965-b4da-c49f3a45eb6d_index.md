## Important cross-file interpretation warnings

1. **Do not confuse files present in the patch with completed work.** The checkpoint board has CP-0…CP-8 all PENDING; the eight handoffs and final report are templates; the traceability results are blank. These documents define expected process/evidence but do not prove implementation or tests.
2. **There are multiple time snapshots.** `PHASE2_MASTER_PLAN.md` is Rev-3 dated 2026-09-12 and its board says PENDING. `PHASE2_SESSION_LEDGER.md` and `PHASE2_SITE_CONTEXT_SUMMARY.md` contain later/historical session claims (including stages beyond CP-8 and phone/audit status). They cannot be merged into one “current status” without checking dated GitHub artifacts and owner rulings.
3. **Old line anchors may be stale.** The session ledger and injected-context dump contain references to an earlier, larger Decision Log; the injected-context dump says it was 1,233 lines. The Decision Log added by this patch has 54 lines. Re-resolve every such anchor in the exact revision before relying on it.
4. **One explicit template anomaly:** `PHASE2_HANDOFF_CP8.md` says its interface list is for CP-9, while the Rev-3 plan defines CP-8 as the last stage. Record/ask about the inconsistency; do not invent a CP-9 stage.
5. **The deleted plan and retained PROMPT body are not active instructions.** The deleted Persian suggestion proposed an earlier multi-agent split. The PROMPT status banner says its old repository `START EXECUTION` block is superseded. The Rev-3 plan's Upstage target and serial CP chain are the relevant process snapshot, subject to owner/current-repository verification.
6. **The dossier is an evidence handoff, not a certificate.** It distinguishes source-reported findings from what was independently checked, and explicitly leaves full audit-row/probe review and independent device/venue acceptance open.
7. **The patch is a diff, not a folder archive.** Search for a path's `diff --git` header to jump to its section. For a new file, use the hunk's `+1,N` count and strip the leading `+` from each payload line to reconstruct it; do not include patch metadata or lines from the next section.
